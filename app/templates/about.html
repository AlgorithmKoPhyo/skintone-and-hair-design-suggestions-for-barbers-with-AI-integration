Shape & Shade:
AI-based Personalized Hairstyle and Color Palettes Recommendation System

5th Year Graduation Engineering Project (Information Science and Technology)

Shape & Shade is an AI-powered beauty & style recommendation engine that analyzes your unique face shape and skin tone. By combining real-time computer vision, facial landmark geometry, and skin undertone analysis, it delivers personalized recommendations for hairstyles, hair colors, curated wardrobe palettes, and matching jewelry. Discover your most flattering aesthetic effortlessly in one smart digital experience.
