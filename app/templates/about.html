Shape & Shade:
AI-based Personalized Hairstyle and Color Palettes Recommendation System

5th Year Graduation Engineering Project (Information Science and Technology)

Shape & Shade is an AI-powered beauty & style recommendation engine that analyzes your unique face shape and skin tone. By combining real-time computer vision, facial landmark geometry, and skin undertone analysis, it delivers personalized recommendations for hairstyles, hair colors, curated wardrobe palettes, and matching jewelry. Discover your most flattering aesthetic effortlessly in one smart digital experience.

AI Ethics & Privacy Commitment

Building intelligent systems with responsibility, fairness, and utmost respect for user privacy.

Data Privacy

No user photos are stored on our servers. All facial analyses are processed instantly in memory and discarded immediately to ensure complete privacy.

Fairness & Bias

Our models are trained on diverse datasets to minimize demographic bias, striving to provide fair and accurate recommendations across all skin tones and face shapes.

Transparency

AI outputs are strictly recommendations, not mandates. Users retain full control over their style choices. We prioritize transparent and explainable suggestions.
