StyleMe: Shape and Shade

5th Year Graduation Engineering Project (Information and Communication Technology)

StyleMe is an intelligent AI-powered beauty & style recommendation engine. By combining real-time computer vision, facial landmark geometry, and skin undertone analysis, StyleMe delivers personalized haircut and hair color recommendations tailored precisely to an individual's natural facial structure.
